What is the product demo?

It is our real dashboard running on a recorded snapshot, so you can click through every tab without an account. Press Play on the home page, or go to ohlcvlabs.studio/demo. The PIN types itself; any PIN works.

Is the demo live? Does it trade?

No. It is frozen on the date shown in its banner, and every trade in it is a simulated paper trade. Buttons that would place an order or change a setting answer “Demo — nothing was sent”: nothing leaves your browser.

Why are some sections locked?

Backtesting, Intraday Trading and Paper Trading are reserved for members. The public demo shows what they are; members can use them.
