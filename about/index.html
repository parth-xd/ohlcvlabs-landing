About Us

Built from the costs up.

OHLCV Labs builds proprietary trading research software for Indian equities, and judges every idea by what it earns after costs.

What we build

OHLCV is our research dashboard. It brings together the things a trading decision actually rests on, in one place:

  • Signals from machine-learning models trained on decades of market data.
  • News sentiment scored headline by headline, stock by stock.
  • A journal and P&L that count every brokerage and statutory charge.

Why costs come first

A strategy can be right on direction and still lose money. In our own paper trading, every trade in the demo snapshot made money before costs, and charges turned most of them into losses. That gap is what our work is built around: an idea is only good if it survives the costs of acting on it.

How we work

  • Evidence over intuition. A rule stays only if it holds up on data it was not built from.
  • Honest numbers. Net, not gross, is the number that counts, and we show both.
  • Inside the rules. We are not a SEBI-registered adviser and do not give advice; see our Public Policy.
